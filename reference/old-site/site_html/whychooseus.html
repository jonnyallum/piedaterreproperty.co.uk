SUCCESSFUL TENANCY BEGINS WITH A CHOICE

We appreciate that you have a number of letting agents to choose from. Whilst the work that we do is ultimately very similar, we believe that our level of service is not.

Instead of relying solely on our years of experience, we also utilise data and information to ensure that we give you the best possible advice.

Something which we feel is often overlooked by landlords is the costs which prospective tenants will be charged by their agent. Expensive fees can deter prospective tenants and in turn this can be detrimental to the landlord. Whilst we must cover our time and third party disbursements, we believe ourselves to be one of the most competitive in the local area.

marketing strategy

Instead of simply advertising your property, we actively work to ensure that you achieve the highest possible rental.

Before marketing your property, we will advise you of any works which we feel will make your property a more attractive proposition to prospective tenants. Your property will be photographed using a digital SLR camera and the photographs finished on industry standard software. We believe that many prospective tenants will make their decision to view your property based upon what they see, which is why we endeavour to ensure that your property is captured at it's best.

One of our distinctive 'To be Let' boards will be placed at the property (where possible). Not only could this help to attract the interest of a home hunter, it also helps viewers to find the property. We will also prepare property particulars with concise and considered information.

We contact our database of applicants who's search criteria matches your property. Your property will be advertised on Rightmove.co.uk, the UK's most popular and best known property website.

WE MATCH PEOPLE TO HOMES

Since 2011, we have strived to find the right people for every property. We work to ensure the prospective tenants accommodation needs will be met in both the short term and the long term. This can increase the longevity of the tenancy and maintain a more consistent rental income for the landlord.

 

our team

Should you choose to place your property with us, you can do so with confidence. Our team has many years experience which includes backgrounds within both independent and corporate letting agencies.

 

MAINTAINING STANDARDS

Your property will be in good company. We take pride in our portfolio.

We encourage our landlords to offer their property for let in the best condition possible. Furthermore, should we continue on to manage the property, we actively work with the landlord and the tenant to ensure that all necessary maintenance is completed to a high standard and in a timely manner.

We believe that if an agent maintains a reputation for offering quality homes with a reliable maintenance schedule, this results in a quicker let to a better tenant who will be happier and likely remain in the property for longer.

diligence

All prospective tenants must pass our referencing process. Whilst we find that in the majority of cases there is no cause for concern, on occasions things are not always as they appear. 

Our tenant referencing process includes a credit search plus, where applicable, obtaining references from their former landlord/managing agent. We do our best to ensure there isn't any reason the tenant will be unable to pay their rent.

In addition to this, we offer our landlords the opportunity to meet with the prospective tenant should they wish to do so. We want you to be completely satisfied that we have found the correct people for your property.

The property ombudsman

We are a member of The Property Ombudsman scheme and we abide by their code of practice to ensure our business operates in a fair and professional manner at all times.
