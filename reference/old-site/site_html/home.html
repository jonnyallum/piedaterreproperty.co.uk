Letting Agent

 
 
 

Pied-à-terre Property is an in independent letting agent based in Havant. In addition to the immediate area, we also cover Portsmouth, Waterlooville, Emsworth and Hayling Island.

Pied-à-terre Property have recently won the 2018 - 2019 British Property Lettings Award for Emsworth & Hayling Island. These awards provide agents throughout the UK with an invaluable opportunity to compare the service that they provide against the service provided by their local, regional and national competition.

Thank you for taking a moment to visit our website, we hope you find the information you require.

Should you have any queries, please visit our contact page and a member of our experienced team will endeavour to assist you.

If you are a prospective tenant looking for your new home, please complete our registration form so we can keep you updated of suitable properties.

With our commitment to excellence and the added assurance of TPO membership, you can trust us to help you navigate the letting process with integrity and professionalism. When a dispute arises between a consumer and property professional, The Property Ombudsman are an impartial organisation who will work to resolve the dispute.

We are part of a government approved Client Money Protection Scheme (CMP) called Money Shield. The scheme was set up at the request of The Property Ombudsman and allows us to offer greater protection to our clients. The accounting rules of Money Shield can be viewed by clicking here (opens in new window).
