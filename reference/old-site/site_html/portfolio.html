A SAMPLE OF OUR PORTFOLIO

The Pied-à-Terre portfolio is somewhat varied and includes everything from studio apartments through to substantial detached homes. In addition, we also let and manage commercial units.

Our principle area is from Southsea to Clanfield, but we have successfully let property as far as Southampton and Chichester. If you are a landlord in search of a professional and knowledgeable letting agent, please view our services or contact us.

Below are some properties we have successfully let.

Wallis road, waterlooville

Charming double bay window house in one of Waterlooville’s best regarded roads. Driveway, garage and good size rear garden.

Bevan Road, Lovedean

Immaculate semi detached house with garage. Accommodation includes spacious lounge with doors into the garden, refitted kitchen, three bedrooms and bathroom. Renewed central heating system, redecorated and new carpets throughout.

Monarch Close, waterlooville

Lovely three bedroom link detached house situated in the popular Tempest Avenue area. The landlord has kept this property in very good order including a modern kitchen and neutral decor throughout.. We have managed this property for a number of years.

GOLD Close, waterlooville

Modern and spacious two bedroom coach house. This property benefits from a garage, allocated parking, en-suite and balcony.

The landlord is a returning client and we have successfully let this property on two occasions.

 

Padnell Grange,
Cowplain

Padnell Grange is an impressive residence with an imposing facade. Set within approximately 7 acres of maintained grounds and adjoining the local golf course.

We are honoured to have been given the opportunity to let and manage this outstanding and unusual property.

West Street, Fareham

Surprisingly spacious three bedroom flat which has accommodation split over two levels. Located in the centre of Fareham and benefiting from off road parking. 

We found three prospective tenants for this property.

BARNEY EVANS CRESCENT, COWPLAIN

Two bedroom terraced house in Cowplain. Lounge/diner with French doors leading to a low maintenance garden, fitted kitchen, two double bedrooms with fitted wardrobes, bathroom. Immaculate throughout.

FERRY ROAD, EASTNEY

One bedroom ground floor flat located close to both the shoreline and nature reserve. Accommodation includes hallway, double bedroom with fitted wardrobes, bathroom and open plan lounge/kitchen. Gas central heating and allocated parking. Well presented throughout.

BURITON, PETERSFIELD

Three bedroom detached bungalow which features an impressive vaulted roof kitchen/dining room with French doors leading to a mature rear garden. Situated in the beautiful rural village of Buriton from which you can explore the surrounding countryside and Queen Elizabeth Country Park. The market town of Petersfield is only a short distance away and offers direct trains into London Waterloo Station.

SEA VIEW ROAD, DRAYTON

Spacious three bedroom detached bungalow occupying a corner plot on the slopes of Portsdown Hill. This property benefits from garden to all sides, plus the added benefits of a conservatory and garage. This property is ideal for a couple but also has the space to accommodate a growing family.

Milton Road, Waterlooville

Spacious two bedroom top floor flat located on Milton Road. This property benefits from a balcony and it’s own front door. Outside is a large car park which generally offers ample parking.

Hulbert Road, Waterlooville

Well presented one bedroom first floor retirement flat. Facilities on site include residents lounge, laundry and drying areas, communal garden and house manager. Please note that applicants must be aged 60 years or older.

The Curve, Lovedean

Semi-detached property situated in a quiet, requested Lovedean cul-de-sac. South facing rear garden. Two ground floor bedrooms plus a first floor master bedroom with en-suite shower room and fitted wardrobe cupboards.

Lowland Road, Denmead

Two bedroom house situated in a quiet cul-de-sac location in Denmead. Lounge with under stairs storage cupboard and doors that leads to the fitted kitchen with a dining area and access to the low maintenance rear garden. Two allocated parking bays.
