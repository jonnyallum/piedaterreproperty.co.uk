# Social posts and Google Business Profile copy

Six social posts for Instagram and Facebook, plus a Google Business Profile post,
announcing the sales arm. House voice, British English, no emoji, no exclamation
marks, no invented facts. Pre-launch register. Each carries image direction and
routes to a tracked link.

Tracked destination for all: piedaterreproperty.co.uk/sales (add the campaign
UTM the analytics setup in prompt 11 defines).

---

## Post 1, the announcement

We have some news. After fifteen years letting and managing homes on this coast,
we are opening a residential sales department. The same people, the same local
knowledge, now for selling too. Register your interest at the link.

Image direction: the office frontage at 233 Southampton Road, or a wide, calm
shot of local housing stock in warm natural light. No stock photography.

## Post 2, the why

Selling a home should feel like dealing with someone who lives here too. We know
which streets have parking, which schools people move for, and what a buyer will
actually pay, because we have worked these five towns for fifteen years.

Image direction: a duotone risograph of Emsworth mill pond or Langstone Harbour
at low tide, in indigo and terracotta.

## Post 3, the same person throughout

No call centre. No being handed to a progression team you have never met. When
you ring in week seven, you speak to the person who valued your home in week one.

Image direction: a real, warm portrait of Charisma on location, mid-conversation.

## Post 4, on being open about fees

We think you should be able to see what selling with us costs before you ever
pick up the phone. One clear fee, VAT stated, nothing hidden. That is unusual
around here, and deliberate.

Image direction: a clean, typographic graphic in the brand palette, ochre rule
on bone. No photograph needed.

## Post 5, the free valuation

A valuation with us is free, honest, and carries no obligation. We visit, we look
at the real evidence, and we tell you what we think and why. If the timing is not
right, that is fine.

Image direction: an arch-masked photograph of a local doorway or bay window in
warm light.

## Post 6, the warm-market invitation

If we already let your property, you know how we work. Soon you will be able to
sell it with us too, and your valuation is on us. One conversation, not a cold
start.

Image direction: a duotone of a To Be Let board in situ on a local street.
