# Review request email

Sent after every completed let or sale. The launch plan is explicit that reviews
win valuations, so this goes out every time, promptly, while the experience is
fresh. House voice, British English, no emoji, no invented facts.

The link points at the Google Business Profile review link once Charisma supplies
the Place ID (tracked in ASSET_REQUEST_LIST). Until then, leave the placeholder
and do not send.

---

## After a completed sale

Subject: Thank you, and a small favour

Hello [first name],

It was a pleasure to help you sell [property or road]. I hope the move goes
smoothly and the next chapter is a good one.

If you have a couple of minutes, a short review would mean a great deal to us. We
are a small local firm, and an honest word from someone we have actually helped
is worth more than any advert. It also helps the next person on your street decide
who to trust.

You can leave one here: [Google review link]

Thank you again, and do keep our number. Many of the people we sell for come back
to us as landlords, and the other way round.
