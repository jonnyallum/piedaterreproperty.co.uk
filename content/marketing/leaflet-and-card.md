# Leaflet and card copy for local street farming

Print copy for door drops, in the house voice. British English, no emoji, no
exclamation marks, no invented facts.

Important honesty note. The brief mentions a "we have buyers waiting" card. Under
the section copy rules we do not publish that claim unless there is a genuine
applicant list to back it on the day it prints. Two versions of the card are
below: a compliant version to use now, and the "buyers waiting" version to use
only once a real applicant list exists, confirmed by Charisma.

Every printed piece carries a QR code pointing at a tracked landing page:
piedaterreproperty.co.uk/sales/landlords (add the print campaign UTM from the
prompt 11 analytics setup).
