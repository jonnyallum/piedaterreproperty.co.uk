# For Sale board rider copy

The physical board is local advertising, so it follows the same rules as every
other asset: house voice, British English, no invented claims, no price on the
board that is not on the listing. Options below; pick one rider per board.

---

## Rider options

1. For Sale. A local agent who knows the street.
2. For Sale, by the people who know this coast.
3. Selling with your local lettings agent.

For a board on a property we let, once sales is live:

4. To Let, and now selling too.

Each rider carries the office number, 07756 869185, and the QR code.

## QR destination

The board QR points at piedaterreproperty.co.uk/l/sell, which resolves to the
thinking-of-selling landing page with print attribution. Never point a board at a
"buyers waiting" message unless a genuine, current applicant list exists for that
area, confirmed by Charisma on the day.

## Practical notes

- Keep the phone number legible from the pavement.
- Board advertising is regulated. State no price or claim that is not on the
  listing, and remove the board promptly once the sale completes.
