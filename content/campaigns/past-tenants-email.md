# Past tenants and applicants email

Draft for Charisma to approve or rewrite. A warm-market email to people who
rented through us or registered with us and have since moved on. The angle is
different from the landlord letter: you rented with us, and when you are ready to
buy or sell we would like to help. British English, house voice, no invented
figures, no pressure.

Tracked link: piedaterreproperty.co.uk/l/sell (resolves to the thinking-of-selling
landing page with attribution). Do not send until the Google links and the sales

## Email

Subject: When you are ready to buy or sell, we would like to help

Hello [first name],

You rented through us a while ago, and I hope wherever you are now feels like
home.

I am writing because we have opened a residential sales department, after fifteen
years letting and managing homes on this coast. If you are starting to think
about buying a place of your own, or selling one, we would like to be useful.

We will not send you listings you have not asked for. What we can offer is honest
local knowledge: which streets tend to hold their value, what a fair price looks
like across our five towns at the moment, and the questions worth asking before
you commit to anything. No obligation, and no hard sell.

If that would help, reply to this email or call the office on 01243 468738. You
can also tell us what you are looking for here:
piedaterreproperty.co.uk/l/sell

Either way, it was good to have you as a tenant, and we would be glad to help you
again.

With best wishes,

Charisma Marie Smith
Pied-à-Terre Property
01243 468738
