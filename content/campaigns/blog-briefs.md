# Blog post briefs

Six briefs for the stubbed /blog, each targeting a real local search a vendor,
landlord or tenant actually types. Briefs only at this stage: title, target
query, outline, internal links and word count. Written up later, in the house
voice, with no invented figures. Where a brief needs a local number, it stays a
[CONFIRM] until the firm supplies its own achieved figures or a dated public
source (Land Registry, ONS).

Every post ends with one clear call to action to the free valuation, and links
to two or three genuinely relevant internal pages so the post pulls its weight
for search.
