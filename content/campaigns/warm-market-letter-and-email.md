# Warm-market letter and email

Drafts for Charisma to approve or rewrite. The warm market, our own landlords
and past tenants and applicants, is the cheapest and warmest lead source for the
sales launch. Both variants are written for pre-launch mode. When
`NEXT_PUBLIC_SALES_LIVE` becomes true, switch the bracketed pre-launch lines for
the live lines noted underneath.

All versions route to the tracked landing page `/sales/landlords`, so the
campaign can be measured against instructions won. Nothing here states a fee, a
percentage or a registration that is not yet confirmed.

---

## Printed letter, from Charisma

Dear [name],

After fifteen years letting and managing homes on this stretch of coast, we are
adding something new. We are opening a residential sales department.

I wanted you to hear it from us first, because you are the reason we can. The
trust you have placed in us to look after your property is exactly what we are
building the sales side on. The same care, the same faces, and the same office
you can walk into.

If you ever think about selling, whether that is this year or some way off, I
would like you to have a free market appraisal from people who already know your
property and know these streets. There is no obligation, and no hard sell. Just
an honest figure and a conversation.

[Pre-launch: We are completing our estate agency registrations now. If you
register your interest, we will contact you the week we open, with your
appraisal ready to arrange.]

[Live variant: Our books are open. Call the office or register online whenever
suits you, and we will arrange a time to visit.]

You can register in a minute at piedaterreproperty.co.uk/l/landlords, or
simply call me on 07756 869185.

Thank you, as ever, for your custom over the years.

With best wishes,

Charisma Marie Smith
Pied-à-Terre Property
